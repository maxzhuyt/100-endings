# SCP-7291 — Temporal Correspondence (自筆)

**Object Class:** Euclid (Under Review)

**Special Containment Procedures:** All materials related to SCP-7291 are to be stored in Secure Document Locker 7291-A at Site-19. Researcher Dr. Elaine Kobayashi is suspended from active duty pending psychological evaluation. Updates to this file require Level 4 authorization.

**Description:** SCP-7291 designates a series of handwritten notes, currently numbering seven (7), which have appeared in the possession of Dr. Elaine Kobayashi between 09/11/2024 and 09/22/2024. All notes are written on standard Foundation GSA-spec memorandum paper. Forensic graphology confirms the handwriting as Dr. Kobayashi's with 99.7% confidence. All notes are dated between 10/02/2024 and 10/09/2024 — approximately two to three weeks following their respective discovery dates.

Discovery of the first note is documented below.

---

**Discovery Log — SCP-7291-1**
**Date:** 09/11/2024
**Documenting Researcher:** Dr. Elaine Kobayashi

During routine annual review of personnel files for Section 4-East, I discovered a folded note inside my own sealed file jacket. The jacket's tamper-evident strip was intact. The note read, in full:

> *10/02/2024 — Cell 4E-0713. BREACH. Not a drill. Not a test.*

I verified: Cell 4E-0713 currently houses SCP-████, a Class-II cognitohazardous entity classified as Neutralized since 2019. Containment is maintained as a formality. The cell's environmental readings are nominal. There is no reason for concern regarding this cell.

I am filing this report for completeness. The note is interesting, primarily as a question of access — how it entered a sealed file — rather than as a credible warning.

---

**Incident Log — SCP-7291-2 through SCP-7291-4**
**Date Range:** 09/14/2024 – 09/18/2024

Three additional notes recovered across five days. Discovery circumstances as follows:

- **SCP-7291-2** (09/14): Found inside my locked desk. Second drawer. The drawer had not been opened in three days; I'd been using the digital filing system. Note read: *"10/04 — Do not allow Dr. Parekh to run Procedure 8811 on schedule. Delay by any means."*
- **SCP-7291-3** (09/16): Found in the inner breast pocket of my lab coat while wearing it. I had not removed the coat since 0800. Note read: *"10/06, 14:32 — Corridor 4E will be empty. Go to 0713. The lock code will be 9-0-4-2-1. You need to see what it's doing."*
- **SCP-7291-4** (09/18): Found between pages 412 and 413 of *Erta & Hong, Applied Temporal Mechanics, 3rd Ed.*, a book I was actively reading at the time of discovery. I had turned past those pages not ninety seconds earlier. Note read: *"They're going to tell you it's the entity. It isn't. Trust the handwriting. Trust your hands."*

I initiated controlled testing. Notes were placed inside sealed, argon-filled acrylic containers under 24-hour video surveillance (Cameras 19-4E-07A and 07B). On 09/19, SCP-7291-5 appeared inside Container 3. Camera footage shows no interruption, no access, no anomaly. One frame the container is empty. The next frame (1/30th of a second later) it is not.

I want to note — this is not the part that disturbs me.

The part that disturbs me is that Dr. Aman Parekh is scheduled to run Procedure 8811 on 10/04. I checked. I had not known this before reading the note.

---

**Addendum 7291-A: Psychological Evaluation Summary**
**Evaluating Officer:** Dr. Cynthia Voss, Site-19 Psychiatric Division
**Date:** 09/20/2024

Dr. Kobayashi presents as alert, coherent, and cooperative. No indication of psychotic features, delusional ideation, or dissociative episodes. Cognitive function intact across all standard measures.

However, Dr. Kobayashi's described experiences are consistent with two non-exclusive hypotheses:

**Hypothesis A:** Genuine temporal anomaly. Notes originate from Dr. Kobayashi's future self via an unknown mechanism, possibly related to SCP-████'s reclassification as Neutralized (reclassification may have been premature).

**Hypothesis B:** Ongoing cognitohazardous influence by SCP-████. The entity's file notes its capacity to produce "authored artifacts" — objects that appear to originate from a trusted source, designed to elicit specific behavioral responses in targeted personnel. SCP-████'s Neutralized status has not been independently verified since 2021.

**Recommendation:** Dr. Kobayashi is removed from unsupervised access to Cell 4E-0713 and placed on monitored duty effective immediately. Procedure 8811 will proceed as scheduled.

---

**Personal Log — Dr. E. Kobayashi (Appended Against Advisement)**
**Date:** 09/22/2024

Three new notes today. Found them together, folded inside my pillowcase in my on-site quarters. The quarters I am now locked into for fourteen hours a day.

SCP-7291-6 references a "Dr. Linden" on the response team. There is no Dr. Linden at Site-19. There is no Dr. Linden in the Foundation directory.

SCP-7291-7 describes the layout of Wing 4E-North, including a sub-basement access corridor. Wing 4E-North's sub-basement was filled with concrete in 2017. I helped pour it.

(The handwriting in these is mine. It is *mine*. But the hand that wrote them was shaking so badly that certain letters — the lowercase *e*, the way I cross my *t*'s from left to right — are barely recognizable. Whoever I become, I become her afraid.)

SCP-7291-8 is one line:

> *You were not born in Sapporo.*

I was born in Sapporo. I have the certificate. I have the memory of my mother telling me about the snow that day, the walk to the hospital, the name she almost chose instead. I know this. This is the thing I know.

Unless the thing in Cell 0713 knows how to build a life so carefully that even the person living it can't find the seam.

---

**FINAL ENTRY — SCP-7291-9**
**Date Found:** 09/22/2024, 21:47
**Location:** Taped to the interior of the bathroom mirror. Written in steady, clear handwriting.

> *09/23/2024. 03:00. Go to 0713. Code is 9-0-4-2-1. Open the inner door. You will understand.*

It is currently 22:14.

I have been told that following the notes is precisely the behavioral cascade that SCP-████ was designed to produce — that I would be, in the clinical language Dr. Voss used, "delivering myself." She is not wrong. The logic is sound.

I have also been told, by my own hand, that delay results in breach. That Procedure 8811 is a trigger. That reality is not what I believe. That logic is also sound, or was, until the notes started contradicting the world — or the world started contradicting the notes.

I am aware this document may serve as the last coherent record of my perspective. I am aware that "my perspective" may not be my own. I note the time.
