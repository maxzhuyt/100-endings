Pepper ran down the street, her chest heaving as she struggled to breathe. She had not run this fast or far since she was a kid. Since the day she, Jade, and Sally met to be more precise, twenty years ago. She kicked the thought away as she pumped her legs and arms harder. She needed to be quick and warn Jade. 
        Jade danced around and sang to her favorite K-pop group as she swept the hallway.  The front door flung open. 
        “Sally’s on her way,” Pepper exhaled. She hunched over as she closed the door behind her.
        “What?” Jade shouted as she turned the music off. “What’s going on?” 
        “Sal… Sally is on her way. I just got off the phone with her. She’s on her way,” Pepper blurted out.
        “I’m not letting her in. I told her I needed time,” Jade responded.
        “She’s said she's tired of waiting for you to forgive her,” Pepper huffed. 
        “You have every right to be mad and not talk to her. But I really think you are putting this off just because you don’t like confrontation. No matter what you choose, I am on your side.” Pepper pulled Jade into an embrace. Tears plopped on Pepper’s back. 
        Ding. Dong. The two turned to the door. Jade exhaled and made her way to the door. 
        Sally stood at the door, her eyes wide. “Are you letting me in?” Sally asked. 
        Jade turned to Pepper for support. Jade looked back at Sally and moved to the side. Sally entered. Jade closed the door, and the three moved into the living room. 
        Jade and Pepper sat on the couch. Sally shuffled her feet as she stood in front of the two women. 
        “So, what do you want?” Pepper exclaimed. 
        “We’ve been friends for years. I don’t understand why you two have pushed me away. I miss you,” Sally lamented. 
        Jade wrung her hands. “It’s not for no reason. You just didn’t think I knew.”
        Sally’s once wanting demeanor changed. No longer able to maintain her naiveté, she sat on the closest chair.  “So, what now? I’m sorry. Okay?” Sally said nonchalantly.
        Rage flowed through Jade. Sally always tried to diminish her actions. “You’re sorry?!” 
        “Yes, I am. I know you probably don’t understand. But this really has been hard for me. You two are my best friends, and I couldn’t come to you at all.” Sally said in a small voice.
        Pepper leaned forward. “Are you blaming us? How did you think we would react?”
        “I’m just saying, I needed you guys. This has been hard.” Sally offered.
        Jade jerked up. “No, you’re not saying anything. You’ve been lying for years, and yeah, that’s my fault; I’ve let so much slide. But this is unforgivable. You used our relationship as leverage to get you promoted and undermine my job. But now you want to complain because you can’t do the job. You need someone to hold your hand? Tough! I lost my job. I’m the victim. Not you.”
        “Wow,” Sally said, shocked.
        “Yeah, wow. I think I’m done. Yeah, I’m actually done. I’m not sure what your intention was, but thanks for stopping by. You can leave now.” Jade motioned for Sally to leave.
        “Some friends y'all are.” Sally said as she left.
        Pepper tilted her head and waved to Sally. “See ya.” Pepper squeezed Jade’s shoulder. “How do you feel?” 
        Jade turned to Pepper. “Free.”
