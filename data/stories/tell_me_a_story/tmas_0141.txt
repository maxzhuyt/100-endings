“I can’t believe we already got invited to a party!” Alice exclaimed excitedly. She and Frank had just moved into the building a few weeks prior, and she had been nervous about making friends. They were new to the city, and Alice felt out of place everywhere she went. But when she bumped into their upstairs neighbor, Lupita, the previous morning, Lupita had invited her and Frank to their Halloween party. 
        Meanwhile, in the apartment above them, a different conversation was taking place. 
        “I can’t believe you invited humans to our party, Lupita! Are you trying to get us ostracized again?” Adolphus raged, pacing back and forth through the living room.
        “I just wanted to be friendly, Dolph! It’s been so long since we’ve had anyone new in the building! And they seem like perfectly nice people!” 
        Adolphus scoffed. “The neighbors in our last building were perfectly nice people too, Lupita, until they found out what we are!” He continued pacing, running his hands through his thick, dark hair anxiously. 
        Lupita stood up and approached him. “Dolph,” she said, taking his hands in hers, “I promise. They aren’t going to find out that we’re werewolves, and we aren’t going to be ostracized. I will make sure all of our guests know that Frank and Alice are humans and to keep their identities under wraps. I promise it’ll be okay.” 
        Adolphus sighed and squeezed Lupita’s hands. “You better be right about that. I am tired of being chased out of building after building, Lupe.”
        Later that evening, as the guests arrived, Lupita made sure to pull each pair aside and warn them about Frank and Alice. 
        “Eat your brains in the kitchen and keep them out of sight,” she told Asaka and Khalida. 
        “These people are our friends, not your blood bags," she said to Drake and Heruka. 
        “No casting spells or going invisible,” she said to the fairies, Iridessa and Elida. 
        Once Lupita was confident that all of their guests were going to be on their best behavior, she felt more at ease and allowed herself to relax and enjoy their annual Halloween party. She grabbed a plate from the snack table and nibbled on some carrot sticks while she waited for her new friends to arrive. 
        A half hour later, the party was in full swing. Everyone was mingling, chatting, snacking, and being merry. But Frank and Alice still hadn’t shown up. 
        “You don’t think they’ve changed their minds about coming, do you?” she asked Adolphus fretfully. But, before he could answer, there was a knock at the door. 
        “They’re here!” Lupita squealed, rushing to the door and pulling it open. 
        Alice smiled shyly at Lupita and handed her a bottle of wine. 
        “I wasn’t sure what to bring you, but this is one of my favorites,” she said. 
        Lupita smiled widely at her as she accepted the bottle. “It’s wonderful, thank you! Should I open it now and pour us a couple of glasses?” she asked. Alice nodded and followed her into the kitchen. 
        Once Lupita had poured a glass of wine for Alice and retrieved a beer for Frank, she took them around to introduce them to the rest of the guests. 
        “Nice to meet you,” Heruka said with a thick Hungarian accent. “This is my partner, Drake.” Drake reached out and shook Alice’s hand. Alice pulled back. His hand was freezing! 
        Heruka continued to stare at Alice, her eyes darkening. She was much taller than Alice, with porcelain skin and long, pointed fingernails painted a deep red color. Drake was smaller, but with a similar complexion. They both had long, straight hair the color of charcoal. They looked more like siblings than romantic partners. 
        “Wow, what a distinct accent, Alice said. “Where are you from?”
        “We are from Hungary,” Drake responded, swilling his drink around and taking a big gulp. 
        “How wonderful,” Alice said. “And that wine. Is it good? It’s so dark, it looks like blood!” 
        “Yes, um, it’s a Sangue di Giuda, the, uh, ‘Blood of Judas’ wine,” Lupita responded nervously. “Let’s go meet everyone else, shall we?” Alice waved at Heruka and Drake as Lupita steered them away. 
        “I’m just going to wait here,” Frank said, leaning against the wall next to the snack table. He had barely spoken a word so far, and Alice wasn’t going to let his social awkwardness prevent her from making new friends in this building. 
        “Suit yourself,” Alice said, turning to follow Lupita. 
        “Oh whoa!” exclaimed Alice, as they approached Asaka and Khalida. She peered closely, taking in the greenish hue of Asaka’s skin and the vaguely rotting smell surrounding them. “Look at your costume, it’s so realistic! How did you achieve that smell? Fish guts? And that eye color– are those contacts? Such attention to detail!” 
        Khalida took a step back from Alice’s inquisitorial stare. “Yes, I’m sorry, please excuse me. I need to step into the kitchen for a minute.” Khalida grabbed Asaka’s hand, pulling her out of the room.  
        “Okay, well, would you like to meet Iridessa and Elida? They’re sisters,” Lupita offered, gesturing toward the beautiful, ethereal woman perched on the love seat in the corner of the room. 
        “Yes, of course,” Alice murmured. “They’re breathtaking.” 
        “Hello, Alice, it is lovely to meet you,” Elida said, gingerly shaking Alice’s outstretched hand. 
        “That’s a lovely bracelet,” Iridessa said, admiring Alice’s bangle. 
        “Oh, thank you!” Alice gushed, beaming. She thrust her wrist closer to them. 
        “It’s pure silver! Would you like to try it on?” She asked, eagerly. Elida and Iridessa each recoiled as though they had been burned. The sisters turned away from Alice abruptly, curling into themselves and whispering animatedly to one another.
        “No! We would most certainly not like that!” Elida said. 
        “Ladies, please excuse us. Sorry, come over here, Alice, please. I want to show you something,” Lupita said, hurriedly ushering her away from the women. 
        “Did I offend them?” Alice asked, her voice quivering. 
        “Oh, no, no, of course not. They, uh, they just prefer gold,” Lupita said. 
        “Get away from that window!” They heard a voice yell. Alice and Lupita looked over to see Adolphus pushing Frank away from the curtain he was pulling back. 
        “What’s your problem?” Frank asked. “I just wanted to see the full moon.” 
        “Yeah, no, sorry, mate. Terrible view of the moon from our balcony. Plus, we have a nosy neighbor who peeks in. Just keep the curtain closed, okay?” Adolphus said, standing in between Frank and the curtained window. 
        “Alice, are you ready to go?” Frank said, coming up to her side. He yawned widely, not even bothering to cover his mouth. 
        “Yes, okay,” she said to Frank. She turned back to Lupita. “Thank you again for inviting us. It was a lovely party.” 
        Lupita embraced her briefly, and led them both to the door. 
        “Please, don’t be strangers!” Lupita called, as she stood in the doorway. Alice turned around, waving goodbye. 
        Once they were gone from view, Lupita shut the door behind her, leaning against it. 
        “Well, that went well, don’t you think?” she said to Adolphus, as he approached her. 
        “Ha, yeah, hun, it was great,” he said, laughing. He wrapped his arms around Lupita and kissed her on the forehead. “But, let’s not have them over on a full moon, again, okay?” The other guests laughed, nodding in agreement.
