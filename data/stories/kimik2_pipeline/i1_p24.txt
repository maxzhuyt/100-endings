SCP-XXXX  
Object Class: Keter  

Special Containment Procedures  
Provisional Site-██ has been established in the ruins of ███████ Textile Works, cordon radius 500 m. Mobile Task Force Psi-8 (“Librarians”) rotated every 72 h. Budget overrun 340 %. Global map now resembles a constellation of missing teeth.  

Description  
SCP-XXXX designates a series of wooden doors (thirty-seven at time of writing) manifesting overnight in abandoned industrial structures. Each door bears an identical trio of scratches around the knob—parallel grooves worn to pale wood, as though someone dragged a key-ring back and forth while waiting.  

Addendum 001-EX Exploration Log  
Team: Lt. Harker, Dr. Kowalski, Agent Mbiru.  
Tether length: 200 m.  
(The air smells like the inside of an old card catalogue—before the paper was acid-free.)  

Interior exceeds exterior geometry. Corridor extends 1.2 km; walls of drawers 4 m high. Silence eats radio. Return at 97 m tether; no anomalous entities encountered.  

Addendum 007-EX  
Side corridor lighting fluctuates. Figures in yellow Level-C suits bearing Foundation seal “ARCHIV-SEC” empty shelves onto silent pallet jacks. When pursued, they retreat at walking pace, maintaining 35 m distance. MTF member Ortega identifies his own gait in the retreating column. Report filed under “possible memetic self-avoidance.”
