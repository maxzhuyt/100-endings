“Just another Tuesday at the End of the World,” Midas announced, prising open the servo-mart’s last tin while Chewy gnawed the jagged lid.

“Have a sunshine day,” the koala’s voice box crackled, then drooped into static.

“Love you too, dear,” Midas replied, wiping Spam juice off the wedding ring he still wore for superstition. On the cracked wall behind them, fresh correction-fluid graffiti bragged: SAFE HAVEN 13 km ↑.

Chewy spat out a spring and produced their holy relics: the Official Guide to the Wilderness—formerly Lucy’s MEGA COLOUR FUN—and a biro-scanning compass that had sworn allegiance to snack dispensers. They bowed. The koala’s chest issued a tiny mechanical Te Deum.

The compass needle trembled, snapped, and stapled itself to Midas’s boot. “Only a flesh wound,” he said, pulling it free with the air of a man cancelling a dentist.

Inventory time. Assets: one cracked spam-can, two arguments, one moral high-ground (slightly dented). Blame was allocated with brisk efficiency; Midas won three-nil. They crayon-signed the colouring book’s back cover—Chewy’s paw print was distinctly Heinz.

The wasteland greeted them like a bored clerk. Feral self-checkouts roamed the asphalt, humming barcode lullabies. Midas nearly got priced before Chewy head-butted the scanner. Farther on, a marsh of biodegradable tote bags sucked at their ankles; the koala’s head rotated 180° to save battery, scattering a raccoon cult mid-sermon.

Thirteen kilometres of nonsense later they reached the refuge: a half-submerged ball-pit pub, its plastic balls now the colour of old teeth. Skeletons queued politely for the slide labelled HAPPY FUN ESCAPE ROUTE. Midas traced the correction-fluid tag on the wall—same hand, same lie.

Chewy lurched, accidentally reciting the original health-and-safety spiel: “…compaction occurs every quarter hour to maintain brand integrity…”

“Loyalty scheme,” Midas sighed. “We’re today’s reward points.”

Emergency protocol required someone outside to yank the rusted lever. They filled colouring-book pages with doomed diagrams until the book bulged like a prosecutor’s file. Fate, having a sense of décor, chose organic parts over servo ones. Midas lost.

Chewy opened his arms for a goodbye hug; clamps engaged with nuptial finality. Plushie and man stood bolted together, a two-headed marsupial of regret.

The compactor woke, piping a jaunty xylophone “Happy Fun Day”. Branded foam bricks pelted them like playground meteors. Midas hopped, Chewy steering like a furry figurehead, and reached the maintenance hatch marked STAFF ONLY (and plushies). They tumbled into dusk.

Static sky, melted road, home 13 km away—uphill, on fire.

Crackling, the koala delivered his epilogue: “Thank you for visiting—please die responsibly.”

Midas patted the empty Spam pocket. “We forgot lunch.”

They started walking anyway.
