09:00 — Morning roll-call of the damned.  
The screams rise in perfect 4/4 time, a brass section of agony. I sip drip-brew that tastes of burnt audit forms and yesterday’s scorched fingernails. Around the conference stalagmite, middle-management imps flip through spreadsheets tattooed on flayed skin. My PR intern—rosy cheeks, gold curls, halo polished for the shareholders—recites overnight metrics like a weather report: “Precipitation of despair up two points, schadenfreude holding steady.” She doesn’t flinch when a sinner’s tongue skitters across the floor chasing its own confession. I sign off with a fountain pen full of starlight, ink that still remembers heaven.

Footnote, tiny-typed at the bottom of the minutes:  
*Yes, we have toner. Yes, the Oracle of Delphi is chained to a Dell. Yes, prophecy crashes more than Windows.*

10:17 — Doorway darkens.  
Lilith stands there, one hand on her hip, the other gripping a leash of braided serpents. At the far end wriggles Cerberus’s newest head: a puppy the size of a pony, all slobber and innocence and teeth like cathedral nails. “Returning company property,” she says. The puppy immediately starts teething on my throne’s left claw, sparks skittering across the basalt. We pretend the break-up stuck; we pretend she’s only here for logistics. I almost ask how Limbo’s therapy scene is, but she’d just smirk, so I offer coffee instead. She takes it black, bitter, no sugar—like always.

11:00 flashes red on my calendar: Eternal Torment Review.  
11:30: Stare at Wall.  
I added both last night so the day would look full. The wall is pitted, warm to the touch, recently screamed upon. I watch it anyway. Lilith scrolls through her own calendar: mani-pedi-demon, brunch with Harpies, spin class across the Styx. We are gods who have run out of excuses to leave.

“Trade?” she proposes, eyes reflecting molten rivers below. “Puppy for one minor continent of lust-sinners. They’re soft, easily mortified.”  
I push across a mug that reads WORLD’S #1 DAD in Comic Sans. “Souvenir.”  
She laughs; Hell’s ceiling drips lava in agreement. No deal, no refusal—just the haggle that keeps us talking.

Tour time.  
We promenade the torture tiers like visiting professors. She corrects a demon mis-gouging eyes: “Angle the fork thirty degrees, you’ll save wrists.” I time her punchlines to the split-second screech of sinners. Between us, efficiency and affection are synonyms. The puppy romps after leaking entrails, red yarn for a kitten. I laugh—first since the Inquisition. She pretends not to notice.

14:45 — Structural integrity fails.  
The puppy’s persistent chewing seals fate: throne leg shears, tons of iron crash, splatter quarterly reports, toast a stack of ungraded souls. Smoke smells of burnt hope and toner. Farce in the house of dread.

We need a new core of mortal hope to weld the joint.  
Options:  
1) Lilith skips girls’ night in Limbo, harvests it herself.  
2) I damn one pure heart, doctor’s orders be damned.  
(“Try cruelty moderation,” my infernal physician said. “You’re scorching your own veins.”)  
She weighs friendships versus favors; I weigh burnout versus boredom. Sitcom music unheard.

We solder together, faces inches above glowing metal. Hope sizzles like fat on a griddle. Five seconds drip from an alarm-clock demon’s tongue: 5…4…3…  
Stab? Kiss? We choose neither; the moment cools, hardens, joins the throne—crooked but standing.

18:00.  
She clicks the leash. “I was never here.”  
Descends obsidian stairs, shadow stretching like a farewell letter I can’t bring myself to read. I almost follow. Instead I flip the lobby sign: OPEN ⇄ ETERNAL. The puppy whines. I pour kibble into a crown-shaped bowl, watch sovereignty become dog dish.

Tomorrow, or the day after, or the century after that, the throne will wobble again, the hound will chew, and she will—
