Mum—  
Bell Rock lamp still flashes every 20 sec—ye can set yer watch by it. Wind fair eats the stone & gulls scream like bairns at 3. Parcel of scones arrived soggy but thanks. Back to lamp.  
—J

12 Oct  
Helicopter couldn’t land, sea too greasy. I’m not lonely—only daft folk get lonely when they’ve the whole North Sea to look at. I wish—  
I watch the dark, that’s all. Barometer falling.  
Love,  
J  
P.Sive got engaged—tell her I said slàinte.

4 Nov  
Tide 4.2 m, fog 11 hrs, wind ENE 5. Fog crawls up the tower like it’s looking for a way in. Gourdon arch missing another tooth—soon be gum. Parcel missed again; running low on baccy.  
Send more when convenient.  
—J

Keeper rounds.

Dear Mum,  
No date today—calendar flew into the sea. I feed the skuas; they trade me feathers for bread. Your letters safe in flare tin. Granda’s voice carried farther than any horn—ye told me that. Horn answers back now.  
—Your wee hellum

---------------------------------------------------------------

I ken who ye are noo.  
Gourdon arch gone entire—coast has forgot its own shape, why shouldn’t I? Rigged lamp: B-E-L-L in Morse, your name before Dad’s. Relief boat due—come before spring gales, or after. I’ll leave the light on either way.  
Your wee hellum,  
J
