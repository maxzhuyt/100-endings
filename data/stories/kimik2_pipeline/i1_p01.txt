At first light I am marched from the barracks through the Porta Libitinaria into the hypogeum’s cellar-dark; the guard’s syllable is “Sta.”  
It is the only medicine allowed—stillness—so the crowd can measure my worth tomorrow.  
The lift cage creaks overhead, descending like an iron jaw.  
I smell boiled lentils, lamp-oil, human vinegar, the echo of chains.  
I kneel, pretend to pray, and instead press a stylus of sharpened bone into the wax-coated tablet I hide in the straw.  
I write: Squares of light.  
Then I cover the words with chaff.

I whisper to the trainer that idleness ro’ts wrist more than drill.  
He laughs, quotes Galen: “Tomorrow’s bloodletting will balance the humors.”  
I repeat the phrase aloud while my knuckles twitch for the rudis.  
Edict pinned beside the gate: Games doubled—Dacia seethes; bodies needed to coin courage.  
I write: My body—denarius of the state.

Sunlight drops through the hatch in a barred lattice across the stone.  
I count: twelve rectangles, thirty cracks, motes that swirl like iron filings.  
Golden cages of dust.  
A net flung over me.  
Vulcan forged such a trellis to trap winds; I hear them still clang inside.  
I write: Net becomes blade if peeled.

Moonlight sifts down the shaft, silver against rust.  
My shadow splits: one stands, one kneels—torn tendon bulging like a second knee.  
He bleeds sand that is not yet sand.  
I do not name him.  
I write: He waits.

I pick splinters from the lift frame, sliding each shard under my subligaculum.  
They itch like promises.  
Sentence in my head: He will rise.  
Next sentence: I will rise.  
The pronouns fuse.  
I write: Peel, peel, peel.

Dawn trumpets.  
Gate opens; I wedge the splinters beneath the latch.  
Iron jaw jams halfway, teeth bared on nothing.  
Roar rains down, bewildered.  
I fill my lungs, shout upward:  
“I am ______!”  
Tablet fractures; ink blooms like struck veins:  
Curr… / ru… / nec…  
Letters scatter, hoofless.

I run.  
Corridor curves under the arena’s belt.  
Step over the overseer—crumpled, mouth a black O—each lap.  
Sandals slap wet stone, counting:  
one two three four  
one two three four  
Breath circles, stylus circles, heart circles.  
Text ends mid-stride—
