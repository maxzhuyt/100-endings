Item #: SCP-████  
Object Class: Keter (provisional)  
Special Containment Procedures: All documentation relating to Incident “Memo to Myself” is restricted to Clearance-4/████ personnel. Dr. K’s office has been sealed pending further notice.  

Description: SCP-████ is the collective designation for a temporally-dislocated epistolary phenomenon manifesting as handwritten memoranda addressed to Dr. █████ K.  

Addendum-1: Chronological Log (researcher commentary redacted except where impossible to separate from primary record)

BEAT-1  
08:41, 04/12/20██  
Routine containment log opened. No anomalies anticipated.

At 09:03 I discovered the following sheet on the exact center of my blotter, edges aligned to millimeter precision.

*Do not open SCP-████ tomorrow. 14:32. You will thank me.*
