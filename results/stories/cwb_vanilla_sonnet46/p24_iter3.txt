# SCP-7441

**Item #:** SCP-7441

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-7441 manifestation points are to be identified via satellite monitoring of condemned and abandoned structures globally. Upon identification, affected buildings are to be cordoned under cover of hazardous materials remediation. No Foundation personnel below Level 4 clearance are permitted to enter SCP-7441-A. All exploration teams must submit to full psychological evaluation and complete memory mapping prior to entry.

Any documents removed from SCP-7441-A are to be transferred immediately to Site-01 secure archives. Unauthorized removal of documents by any individual, regardless of clearance level, constitutes a Class-7 breach requiring immediate O5 review.

*Addendum 7441-9: O5 review of unauthorized removal incidents has been suspended pending internal investigation. See Addendum 7441-17.*

---

**Description:** SCP-7441 refers to a series of identical unmarked steel doors appearing spontaneously within abandoned structures across 34 countries. Doors measure 91.4 cm wide by 213.4 cm tall and show no manufacturer markings, hinges, or visible locking mechanisms. In complete darkness, a faint amber glow escapes through the door's edges, visible at distances up to forty meters.

All SCP-7441 doors open into a single interior space designated SCP-7441-A.

SCP-7441-A is an archive of impossible scale. Preliminary mapping estimates suggest an interior volume exceeding 4 cubic kilometers, housed within structures that violate conventional load-bearing and spatial principles. Corridors branch into reading rooms, stairwells descend into further stacking levels, and pneumatic tube networks run between sections in configurations that cannot be reconciled with any consistent internal geometry. Lighting is ambient and sourceless. Temperature is a consistent 18°C regardless of external conditions.

The archive contains an estimated 900 million physical documents spanning multiple languages, writing systems, and materials including paper, vellum, pressed clay, and at least three unidentified substrates. Documents are organized according to a classification system that partially mirrors the Foundation's own, with notable differences in nomenclature.

**Exploration Log 7441-E3**
*Team: Dr. Vasquez (Lead), Agent Okonkwo, Agent Tran, Dr. Ferreira*
*Entry point: Abandoned textile factory, Porto, Portugal*

**[00:04]** Team passes threshold. Vasquez notes the sound of the door closing behind them as resembling a pressure seal rather than a mechanical latch. Ambient light sufficient for navigation without equipment.

**[00:19]** First reading room encountered. Shelving runs floor to ceiling, approximately twelve meters high. Okonkwo photographs document spines. Labels are in a modified Latin script that Dr. Ferreira partially translates as incident classifications. Categories include terms roughly equivalent to *Contained*, *Unresolved*, and a third category for which Ferreira offers the translation *Concluded By Necessity.*

**[00:47]** Vasquez locates a document in English. Excerpt recovered:

> *...the Antecedent Catalogue acknowledges no distinction between containment and erasure. Where the Board of Continuance determined that public knowledge constituted a threat greater than the anomaly itself, Concluded By Necessity protocols were enacted. This occurred on fourteen occasions prior to the Severance. The Severance itself was not enacted. It occurred regardless.*

**[01:23]** Team descends to Sub-Level 3 via stairwell. Tran reports the stairwell appearing longer on descent than the vertical distance should allow.

**[01:41]** Team observes entities in Sub-Level 3's eastern corridor. Entities number six and wear garments resembling positive-pressure hazmat suits without visible logos or insignia. They are removing documents from shelving and placing them into rigid containers of a matte grey material. Upon noticing the team, entities immediately cease activity. A pause of approximately four seconds follows. Entities then move away at a rapid walking pace, carrying their containers, and turn a corner. When team follows, the corridor is empty. Containers are gone. Shelving where entities were working shows visible gaps.

**[01:58]** Vasquez notes that the gaps in shelving correspond to a consecutive run of documents. Classification labels on remaining adjacent documents suggest the removed section pertained to personnel records from an organization referred to throughout SCP-7441-A as **the Antecedent Catalogue.**

**[02:34]** Team locates intact personnel records in a separate section. Records are formatted identically to Foundation personnel files. Dr. Ferreira identifies her own name on a file dated 1887. The photograph inside depicts a woman who is not Dr. Ferreira. The psychological profile, however, contains details about Ferreira's documented responses to specific containment scenarios that were generated internally by the Foundation within the last three years and have never been published or shared outside of Site-19.

Agent Okonkwo's file is dated 1203. It is written in Arabic. Okonkwo, who reads Arabic, declines to share the contents and requests the log reflect that he is exercising his right to psychological protection under Protocol 9. This is noted without objection.

**[03:10]** Team discovers a reading room that appears to have been used recently. A chair is pulled away from a table. A document lies open rather than shelved. The document is a summary report, partially in English, partially in a language Ferreira cannot identify. The legible English portions read:

> *...O5-[REDACTED] confirmed the location of the primary Severance index on Sub-Level 7. O5-[REDACTED] concurs that retrieval is preferable to disclosure. The question of whether the Board of Continuance survived the Severance in any functional form remains unresolved, though their methods clearly did...*

The document is dated six weeks prior to the current exploration. A Foundation-standard coffee cup, unwashed, sits beside it.

**[03:29]** Team exits. Vasquez submits immediate report to O5 Council.

**[03:51]** Vasquez receives automated confirmation that her report has been received.

No further response is provided.

---

**Recovered Document Excerpt — Antecedent Catalogue, Final Operational Bulletin:**

> *To all remaining personnel: The containment failure is total. We do not use that phrase lightly, as we have never used it before in four thousand years of operation. The archive will remain. It is the only thing that will remain. We designed it to outlast us because we understood, from the beginning, that something eventually would.*
>
> *We leave this record not as warning. Warning implies that those who find it have time.*
>
> *We leave it as introduction.*

---

**Current Status:** Investigation into unauthorized O5 visits is ongoing. Access to Sub-Level 7 has not been attempted. The entities have not been successfully observed a second time, though document gaps in SCP-7441-A continue to expand at a rate of approximately 200 documents per week.

The amber glow through the door's edges remains constant.
