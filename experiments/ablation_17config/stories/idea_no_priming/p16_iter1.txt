# The Surviving Audio Diaries of Gerald Pfeiffer, Certified Flush Auditor

---

## EDITOR'S NOTE

*The following transcripts are drawn from the fourteen surviving analog microcassettes of Gerald R. Pfeiffer (Bureau ID: FA-4409-GL), recovered during the 2037 decommissioning of the Bureau of Biological Compliance's Northeast Regional Processing Center. Of an estimated thirty to thirty-five original recordings, twenty-one are unaccounted for. Tapes 1 through 4 have never been located. Tape 9 was confiscated by the Bureau's Internal Suppression Office during the Pfeiffer proceedings and is presumed destroyed per standard protocol. Tape 11 is water-damaged beyond salvage. Tape 17 was recorded over — apparently by a subsequent occupant of the storage unit — with what audio analysts have identified as sixty-eight minutes of the Bureau-mandated Bowel Affirmation Cycle, a guided recitation exercise once distributed to Compliance Tier employees. ("I release with gratitude. I release with purpose. My output is my gift to the monitored community.") We have been unable to determine whether the overwriting was accidental or policy.*

*The remaining tapes were authenticated in 2039 by three independent audio forensics laboratories, cross-referenced against Gerald Pfeiffer's Bureau voiceprint (still on file, as federal law prohibits the deletion of biometric data associated with Rogue Voider proceedings, even posthumously). They are presented here in their probable chronological order, with minor edits for intelligibility. Gaps in the narrative are noted where they occur.*

*The Bureau of Biological Compliance was dissolved by legislative act in 2041. Director Maureen Trask's subsequent indictment and trial are a matter of separate public record.*

*These tapes are released as part of the Bureau Records Transparency Initiative. The Initiative's editorial board neither endorses nor disputes any claims made by Gerald Pfeiffer in these recordings. Certain statements may reflect the distorted perceptions of an individual under extreme psychological duress. Listeners are encouraged to consult the official Bureau post-dissolution FAQ, available at compliancearchive.gov, for context.*

*— Dr. Anika Feld, Chief Archivist, Bureau Records Transparency Initiative, 2043*
