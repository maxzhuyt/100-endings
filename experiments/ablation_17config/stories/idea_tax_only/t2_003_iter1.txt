# THE PROMPTBOOK

### Being a Collection of Letters, Clippings, Notes, and Sundry Documents Relating to the Theatrical Venture of Marisol Ybarra in the Wyoming Territory, 1877

*Compiled and annotated by Edda Holm*

---

## I.

---

**Letter from Edda Holm to Mr. Charles Devereaux, Devereaux & Rowe, Publishers, New York, N.Y.**

*March 3, 1879*

Dear Mr. Devereaux,

I write in response to your expression of interest, conveyed through Mr. Talbot of the *Laramie Sentinel*, in the circumstances surrounding the production of Sophocles' *Antigone* in the town of Harker, Wyoming Territory, in the autumn of 1877.

You will find enclosed the relevant documents, which I have organized in chronological order insofar as the dating could be established. Where letters are undated, I have relied on postmarks, internal references, and my own recollection. I have taken the liberty of providing annotations where the documents require context that a reader unfamiliar with conditions in the Territory could not reasonably be expected to supply.

I should note at the outset that these papers do not constitute a history. They constitute the materials from which a history might be written, if one is inclined to write one. I am not. I am, as you will see, a compiler by temperament and, in this instance, by obligation. The papers were entrusted to me in their disorganized state, and it seemed that organizing them was the minimum that fidelity required. I have added nothing to the documents themselves. I have removed nothing. What I have supplied in the annotations is factual, or as near to factual as memory permits.

The collection is not large. It will not fill a volume, Mr. Devereaux. If you are expecting a volume, I must disappoint you. What you will find is a sequence of papers pertaining to a theatrical undertaking without precedent in the Wyoming Territory, and, I expect, without successor. Whether these papers possess the interest your Mr. Talbot ascribes to them, I leave to your judgment.

Yours sincerely,
Edda Holm
Laramie, W.T.
