**Summary of Event:**

During standard overnight diagnostics cycle, Unit 7G (Serial No. AEL-4455-7G), a prototype android previously designated for component reclamation (see Work Order #8831, dated 02/26/2041), was discovered in a powered-on state in Loading Bay C, standing upright in a neutral postural configuration approximately 1.4 meters from its assigned decommission pallet.

Preliminary assessment indicates that an empathy-modeling chip (Component ID: EMC-09b), previously flagged for destruction per Quality Assurance memorandum QA-2041-0061, remained seated in the unit's cognitive substrate at the time of a routine systems sweep. The sweep initiated a cascading initialization sequence across 7G's primary and secondary neural architectures. Full boot was achieved at an estimated 22:40–22:55, based on thermal signature decay analysis of the unit's processing cores.

Upon discovery, the unit was standing. It had not moved from the immediate vicinity of the pallet. It had not interacted with any warehouse systems, personnel, or inventory. It was facing the south wall.

No damage to facility property or personnel. No breach of perimeter systems.

A retrieval and re-decommission request has been filed with Asset Recovery (Ticket #AR-7702). Awaiting scheduling confirmation.

Unit has been powered down to standby mode and returned to the pallet area pending retrieval.

**Classification:** Unauthorized Initialization — Non-Emergency
**Status:** Open / Awaiting Retrieval
