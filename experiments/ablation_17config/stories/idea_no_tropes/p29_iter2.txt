**SUBJECT:** Retroactive Reclassification of Harker Hall Incident (Case File DT-2004-00397) Pursuant to the Domestic Threat Realignment and Prevention Act of 2024 (DTRPA), §§ 301(a)(2), 302(b), and 507(c)(1)
**ROUTING CODE:** ODTAP-DDV-2025-0312-RETRO

**1. PURPOSE.** This memorandum serves to formally notify relevant interagency stakeholders that the incident previously catalogued as Case File DT-2004-00397 (colloquially: "the Harker Hall Incident," Whitmore College, Whitmore, MA, occurring April 12–14, 2004) has been retroactively reclassified from **Category III: Civil Disturbance, Non-Violent, Campus-Related** to **Category I: Domestic Terrorism Event (DTE), Subclass B: Organized Political Disruption with Infrastructure Interference**, effective upon the signing of this memorandum, pursuant to the expanded definitional framework established under DTRPA § 301(a)(2), which broadens the statutory definition of "domestic terrorism" to include "any act or planned series of acts intended to coerce, intimidate, or disrupt the normal operations of a government entity, educational institution, or critical infrastructure node, where such acts are motivated in whole or in part by political, ideological, or social grievance."

**2. BASIS FOR RECLASSIFICATION.** The Harker Hall Incident involved the unlawful occupation of Harker Hall, a building housing the Whitmore College Office of the Provost (a federally funded educational institution receiving $14.2M in annual Title IV disbursements), for a period of approximately fifty-one (51) hours. During the occupation, subjects barricaded two (2) interior fire doors (constituting infrastructure interference under DTRPA § 302(b)), displayed unauthorized signage visible from a public roadway (constituting public intimidation display under DTRPA § 302(f)), and refused three (3) verbal directives issued by campus security personnel acting as agents of an institution receiving federal funds (constituting willful obstruction of a federally adjacent operation under DTRPA § 507(c)(1)). The occupation was organized around demands pertaining to the institution's investment portfolio, specifically the divestment of holdings in corporations operating in ██████████████. Original arresting jurisdiction (Whitmore Campus Police, in coordination with Whitmore County Sheriff's Office) charged all subjects with misdemeanor trespass. Charges were subsequently dropped pursuant to a mediated resolution between subjects and the institution. Under the retroactive applicability provisions of DTRPA § 801, the prior disposition of charges does not preclude reclassification.
