# THE COMPLETE ADMINISTRATIVE RECORD OF RELAY STATION AURIGA

## Compiled from recovered documents, digital correspondence, and personal effects.

## Provincial Reconstruction Archive, Case File 2024-0193.

---

*The following materials were recovered from Relay Station Auriga, a logistics and communications waypoint operated by the Joint Provincial Reconstruction Authority (JPRA), located 140 kilometers southeast of the Provincial Capital in Sector 7-November. The station was staffed by a rotating complement of between nine and fourteen personnel, comprising military liaison officers, civilian logistics coordinators, and contracted support staff. The documents span a period of nineteen days. They have been arranged in chronological order. Gaps in the record are noted where they occur.*

*The first document is dated eleven days before the final transmission.*

**INTER-OFFICE MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** All Auriga Personnel
**FROM:** Station Director P. Langford
**DATE:** March 3
**RE:** Kitchen Protocols (REVISED)

It has come to my attention that the shared refrigerator in the common area is again being used to store personal items in excess of the allocated shelf space (one half-shelf per person, as outlined in the Auriga Station Handbook, Section 4.2.3). I will not name names. I will note that an entire shelf is currently occupied by a single individual's collection of hot sauces, at least three of which appear to have expired.

Additionally: the coffeemaker is a shared resource. If you empty the pot, you make a new pot. This is not a suggestion. This is the foundational compact of civilized cohabitation.

Please also note that the weekly all-hands meeting has been moved from Thursday 0900 to Wednesday 1600, effective immediately, to accommodate the new satellite uplink schedule.

Thank you for your cooperation.

P. Langford
Station Director, Relay Station Auriga, JPRA

**MINUTES — AURIGA STATION ALL-HANDS MEETING**

**Date:** March 5, 1600
**Present:** Langford (chair), Marsh, Cota, Yates, Dr. Fenne, Okonjo, Russert, Tariq, Beale
**Absent:** Volkov (overnight shift), Singh (overnight shift)

**1. Opening Remarks (Langford):** Langford welcomed the group to the new Wednesday time slot. No one appeared welcoming.

**2. Supply Update (Yates):** Yates reported that the Feb 28 convoy has been rescheduled to March 9 (tentative). Diesel reserves are adequate. Coffee filter situation described as "getting philosophical." Langford asked Yates to submit a supplemental justification for the generator belt. Yates asked if the three previous submissions were insufficient. Langford said procedure is procedure.

**3. Communications (Okonjo):** Satellite uplink functioning normally. Okonjo noted intermittent signal degradation on the southern relay but attributed it to weather. Daily check-ins with Provincial HQ and with Relay Stations Boreas and Dione proceeding on schedule. Okonjo noted that Boreas was twelve hours late on their last check-in but said this was "not unusual for Boreas."

**4. Security (Marsh):** Marsh reported no incidents. Perimeter sensors operational. Marsh noted that the motion sensor on the southeast quad has been triggering intermittently — likely local wildlife. Cota volunteered to inspect. Marsh requested, again, that the station's two exterior floodlights on the south face be repaired. Langford noted this would require a work order submitted through the Facilities portal.

**5. Medical (Dr. Fenne):** Dr. Fenne reported no health concerns. Reminded personnel that the water purification tablets are for the field kits and not for "making the tap water taste interesting." Personnel appeared guilty.

**6. General Business:** Russert raised the coffee protocol issue. Brief but spirited discussion followed. Langford reiterated the policy. Beale said the policy assumed "a baseline level of human decency that this station has not demonstrated." Meeting adjourned 1638.

**Next meeting:** March 12, 1600.

---

*Nine personnel attended the March 5 meeting. Two were on overnight shift. The station's assigned complement at this date was eleven. A twelfth staff member, contracted interpreter Lena Volkov, is listed on the overnight-shift roster but does not appear in any subsequent document until March 14. The reason for this gap is not explained in the administrative record. The personnel file for Relay Station Boreas, which was twelve hours late on its scheduled check-in, was subsequently transferred to Case File 2024-0187. That file is sealed.*

**PERSONAL JOURNAL — CPT ELENA MARSH**

**March 7 (evening)**

Supply convoy pushed again. Now "tentatively" March 11. Yates is tracking diesel by the gallon. Langford doesn't seem concerned, or doesn't seem to want to seem concerned, which around here amounts to the same thing.

Can't raise Boreas. Three days now. Okonjo says Provincial isn't responding to his flags — just automated acknowledgments. I looked at the map today. Boreas is ninety klicks north. Dione is sixty klicks east. We're the middle point of a triangle, and two of the three points have gone either silent or strange.

Probably nothing. Boreas has had comm issues before. And Dione's brevity could be anything — new operator, bad day, orders to minimize transmission. I'm aware that I'm pattern-matching, and I'm aware that pattern-matching in an environment this monotonous is mostly how people go crazy.

Cota fixed the generator belt with a piece of cargo strap and what he says was "structural optimism." It's holding. The man is wasted on the army.

The southeast motion sensor triggered three more times today. Cota checked it — no tracks, no animal sign. He reset it. It triggered again an hour later. He says it's probably a fault in the sensor itself. Maybe. Sensors don't usually develop fault patterns that only occur between 0200 and 0400.

I should write this up. I will write this up. But write it up as *what?* "Dear Director Langford, I have a feeling"? He'll tell me to submit it through the Concerns portal and file a sensor maintenance ticket.

I'm going to sleep. Tomorrow I'll be less paranoid. This place does this to everyone eventually.
