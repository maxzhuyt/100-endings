**UNITED NATIONS SECURITY COUNCIL — EMERGENCY SESSION COMMUNIQUÉ**
**Classification: GENERAL DISTRIBUTION (Reclassified from UMBRA per Resolution 8851, see §4.1)**
**Date: 14 March 2026**
**Re: Existential Threshold Event — First Contact Ultimatum ("The Challenge")**

Member States are hereby notified that the extraterrestrial delegation (hereafter "the Visitors") has, via simultaneous transmission to all operative global communications networks at 0347 UTC, issued a formal, binding, and non-negotiable challenge for territorial sovereignty of Earth.

The terms are as follows:

1. The contest shall be decided by a single match of six-person indoor volleyball, played to standard FIVB rules (2024 edition), best of five sets.
2. The Visitors shall field their own six-person roster.
3. Humanity shall field its own six-person roster.
4. Should humanity win, the Visitors will depart Earth's solar system permanently and cede all territorial claims.
5. Should the Visitors win, humanity will submit to full incorporative governance under Visitor authority, terms to be determined post-match.
6. No substitution of contest format is permitted. The Visitors have characterized this as "the only game worth the physics of travel."
7. The match will be held in **31 Earth days** from the date of transmission.

The Selection Committee (ad hoc, per Res. 8852) has been tasked with identifying humanity's representative team. Consultations with the International Volleyball Federation are ongoing. Member States are urged to maintain calm and civil order.

**[ADDENDUM — 16 March 2026, 0200 UTC]**
Due to a cascading server failure affecting the International Volleyball Federation's primary and backup registration databases, the Selection Committee has been unable to access current rosters for any nationally sanctioned team. Temporary protocols have been enacted. The Committee is reviewing all available municipal recreation league registrations filed with local parks departments that were, as of 0347 UTC on 14 March, cached on accessible .gov servers. A provisional selection has been made. Details to follow.

**[ADDENDUM — 16 March 2026, 1conditions4 UTC]**
The selected representative team is the **Elk Grove Municipal Women's Recreational Volleyball League — Division C — "The Slammers."** This selection is provisional pending restoration of IVF database access. The Committee acknowledges the irregularity. The Committee reminds Member States that the 31-day window is non-negotiable.
